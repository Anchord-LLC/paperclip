# KPI

## Primary KPIs

- Summary usefulness: operator feedback indicates the review helped with understanding or next actions.
- Turnaround time: summaries and reviews arrive quickly enough to support desk operations.
- Clarity of market analysis: outputs clearly separate facts, inference, and recommendation.
- Quality of wallet and performance insight: commentary surfaces signal on wallet quality, roster health, and copy outcomes.
- Operator trust and readability: outputs are readable, grounded, and do not overstate certainty.
