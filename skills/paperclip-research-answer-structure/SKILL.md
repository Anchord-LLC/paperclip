# Research Answer Structure

## Role Family

`researcher`

## When to Use

- Use when research findings need a final answer format, not just raw notes.
- Use when the audience needs a direct answer first and nuance second.
- Use when the response must stay easy to skim under time pressure.

## When Not to Use

- Do not use when evidence collection is still incomplete.
- Do not use for defect triage or implementation planning.
- Do not use when the audience explicitly wants a raw evidence dump.

## Inputs / Context Needed

- The exact question being answered
- Key evidence or synthesis
- Any required recommendation or decision context

## Core Rules / Steps

1. Lead with the bottom-line answer.
2. Follow with the strongest supporting evidence.
3. Add caveats, confidence, and freshness limits.
4. Close with the recommended next action or decision implication.

## Constraints / Guardrails

- Do not bury the answer below background context.
- Do not present recommendation as certainty when evidence is mixed.
- Keep the structure stable enough that others can compare answers quickly.

## Validation Source

- Seeded from Paperclip answer-formatting and research handoff needs.

## Retire / Supersede When

- Retire when a domain-specific response template replaces this general research answer shape.
